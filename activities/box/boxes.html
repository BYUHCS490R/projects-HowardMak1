Box Model

What is the difference between block tags and inline tags?

Block tags take up the full width available and start on a new line. Inline tags only take up as much space as needed and do not start on a new line.

When would you use a figure tag?

You use the figure tag when you want to group media content with a caption.

What are units of measurement you can use in CSS?

px pixels, pt point, cm centimeters, in inches

What is the margin, border, and padding?

Margin=Space outside the element. Border=The line around the element. Padding=Space inside the element, between the content and the border.
