ingredients

  • 3 Cup Rice
  • 100 ml cup coconut milk
  • 3 and a half cups Water
  • 8 sheets Lime leaves remove the stems and finely chop
  • 1 section Galangal
  • 3 sticks lemongrass
  • 5 cloves red onion
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 2 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp Mushroom Broth

    How to cook

  1. Prepare the ingredients. Peel the onions, slice the lime leaves, and roughly chop them. Wash the rice thoroughly.
  2. Put the rice into the rice cooker pan, add all the spices, coconut milk and water.
  3. Cook for 45 minutes in a rice cooker until cooked through. Stir well.
  4. Heat for 10 minutes in a rice cooker. The rice is ready to eat.
