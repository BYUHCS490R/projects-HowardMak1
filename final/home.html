Interesting Facts

  • While it can be eaten at any time, Nasi Uduk is iconic as a morning dish.
  • Nasi kuning is historically used in Indonesian culture to symbolize gratitude and a "golden" future.
  • Lime leaf rice is not a traditional specialty from any specific region in Indonesia, but rather a modern culinary innovation that has gained popularity in recent times.
