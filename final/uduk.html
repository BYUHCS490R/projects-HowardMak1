ingredients

  • 2 cups rice
  • 1 cup water
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • 2 Bay leaves
  • 1 lemongrass
  • 5 cm galangal/ginger
  • 1 tsp salt

    How to cook

  1. Wash the rice 3 times or until the water slightly clearer.
  2. In a medium pot, put rice, water, coconut milk, bay leaves, galangal, lemongrass,and salt, stir and cook in medium heat until the water reduced.
  3. While the rice cook, prepare steamer pot, let it boil.
  4. Once the water reduce, move the rice to the steaming basket and steam for 20-30 minutes.
  5. Once it's done, serve it.
